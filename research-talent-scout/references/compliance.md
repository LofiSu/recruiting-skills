# Public researcher-data boundaries

## Allowed

- Use public professional pages, official scholarly records, personal CVs, institution or laboratory profiles, GitHub, and OpenReview.
- Use the public OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar metadata APIs. Send `OPENALEX_API_KEY` or `S2_API_KEY` only as request headers, and stay within the published rate limits.
- Record public professional homepages and contact routes only when a directly accessible page supports them.
- Preserve missing data, ambiguity, conflicts, source failures, timestamps, and coverage gaps.

## Forbidden

- Do not guess email addresses or collect private, personal, or non-professional contact information.
- Do not log in, solve CAPTCHAs, scrape restricted platforms, or bypass robots rules, paywalls, or access controls.
- Do not treat search snippets or same-name profiles as verified identity evidence.
- Do not rank candidates, make employment decisions, or send outreach.

Results are research leads for human review. Every non-empty professional fact must retain its evidence URL.
